The Euclid Q1 Galaxy Explorer is an interactive tool for inspecting galaxy
cluster members, combining [Zoobot](https://github.com/mwalmsley/zoobot)
morphology predictions with physical properties measured from Euclid data.

The catalogue can be filtered on physical properties — photometric redshift,
stellar mass, cluster-centric distance `r/r500` and local projected density —
and on the Zoobot morphology fractions, such as the smooth and featured
fractions, bar probability, edge-on fraction and the presence of spiral arms.
The **Scatter Plot** page puts any two of those morphology columns on the axes
and lets you lasso or box-select galaxies to inspect them individually, and each
galaxy's coordinates open directly in ESASky.
